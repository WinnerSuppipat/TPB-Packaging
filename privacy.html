Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

Last updated: September 3, 2026

TPB Packaging Co., Ltd ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website tpbpackaging.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Contact form on our Site, we collect the following information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Message content

We do not collect this information through any other means beyond what is described in Section 5 below.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit through the Contact form solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Communicate with you about our products and services, if relevant to your inquiry

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Information submitted through our Contact form is used only to respond to your inquiry. We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

4. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct or delete your information
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you

To make any of these requests, please contact us at tpbi_mk@hotmail.com.

5. Cookies

This Site uses cookies through Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors use our Site, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors found us. This information helps us improve our Site and content.

Google Analytics cookies only load after you provide consent through the cookie banner shown when you first visit our Site. If you decline, no Analytics cookies will be set. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's cookies and site data for this Site, which will cause the banner to appear again on your next visit.

The information collected through Google Analytics is processed by Google in accordance with their own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not use this data to personally identify you.

If we add any additional cookies or tracking technologies in the future, we will update this section accordingly.

6. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites (such as social media pages). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
